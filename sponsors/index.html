For research sponsors

A quality research site, ready for your next study

We offer our sponsors a trained and experienced clinical research team. The diverse Palm Beach County suburban population, exceeding 1.3 million, supports FDA standards for ethnically and racially diverse subjects, and our central location near major highways and public transportation keeps access easy for both subjects and sponsor visits.
