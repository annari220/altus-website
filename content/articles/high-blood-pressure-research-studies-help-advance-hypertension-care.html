Located in Lake Worth, FL, and Laurel, MS, Altus Clinical Research is committed to improving health outcomes through innovative clinical trials. Our current study is dedicated to exploring new approaches for managing high blood pressure (hypertension) in adults.

What Is High Blood Pressure?

High blood pressure, or hypertension, occurs when the force of blood against your artery walls is consistently too high. Over time, this condition can lead to serious health problems, including heart disease, stroke, and other complications. Managing high blood pressure is essential for long-term health, and research studies like ours play a crucial role in discovering better treatments.

Who Can Participate in This Study?

You may qualify for our high blood pressure research study if you meet the following criteria:

  • You are a man or woman aged 18 years or older

  • You have mild to moderately high blood pressure

  • You are currently taking 0 or 1 blood pressure lowering medication

  • You do NOT have kidney disease, liver disease, or uncontrolled diabetes
    (Uncontrolled diabetes means your blood sugar levels are not within the ideal range despite using medication)

  • You do NOT have a history of cardiovascular disease
    (Examples include unstable angina, heart attack, or stroke)

  • You do NOT have a current infection or related illness

  • You are able to attend 3 trial visits at our Lake Worth research site over approximately 6 weeks
    (Each visit will take about 1–2 hours)

Additional eligibility requirements may apply and will be discussed during your screening process.

Why Join a High Blood Pressure Clinical Trial?

By participating, you can:

  • Contribute to important medical research that may improve hypertension care for yourself and others

  • Receive care and monitoring from a dedicated team of clinical research professionals

  • Gain access to potential new therapies for high blood pressure

The Importance of Clinical Research

Clinical trials are vital for discovering new therapies and improving existing treatments. They allow researchers to evaluate the safety and effectiveness of new approaches under carefully controlled conditions. At Altus Clinical Research, we are proud to conduct high-quality studies that help advance the field of medicine and support better health for our community.
