Living with moderate to severe plaque psoriasis can be challenging. The persistent redness, itching, and scaling affect not just your skin but your confidence and quality of life. Fortunately, medical research is continuously advancing to bring new and improved treatments to those who need them. At Altus Clinical Research in Lake Worth, FL, patients have the unique opportunity to be part of cutting-edge clinical trials focused on psoriasis, helping advance science while potentially benefiting their own health.

What Is a Psoriasis Clinical Trial and Why Participate?

Clinical trials are studies designed to evaluate new treatments under expert care in a controlled and safe environment. These studies help researchers and doctors understand whether new medications or therapies are effective and safe for people living with conditions like psoriasis.

Participating in a clinical trial not only allows you to gain access to innovative therapies that might not yet be available to the public but also provides comprehensive health monitoring and support from a dedicated medical team. Altus Clinical Research prioritizes patient safety, comfort, and respect throughout every step of the process.

Who Can Join the Psoriasis Study?

Altus Clinical Research is currently enrolling volunteers for a psoriasis study. You may be eligible to join if you:

  • Are between the ages of 18 and 75
  • Have been diagnosed with moderate to severe plaque psoriasis

Additional criteria apply to ensure personalized and appropriate care, which will be discussed during a screening process by the clinical team.

What to Expect When You Join the Study

When you volunteer for the study, you will undergo thorough health screenings, regular check-ins, and receive study-related medications or treatments, all under the supervision of experienced clinical researchers. The team at Altus seamlessly integrates medical expertise with compassionate patient care to create a supportive environment for participants.

Throughout the trial, participants receive free health assessments, laboratory tests, and diagnostic services related to the study. Your time and commitment are valued, and compensation may be provided to help cover travel or other expenses.

The Altus Clinical Research Advantage

Altus Clinical Research is a privately owned, South Florida-based research site with extensive experience in all phases of clinical trials. Led by a veteran team of investigators and coordinators, the center collaborates with community physicians to provide easy access to trials that can make real differences in patients’ lives.

Patient dignity and safety are central to Altus’s mission. By partnering with major pharmaceutical corporations, Altus is at the forefront of testing innovative treatments that may one day become standard care for psoriasis and other conditions.

How Participation Can Help You and Others

By joining this psoriasis clinical trial, you are not only taking a proactive step toward managing your own health but also contributing to medical advancements that could benefit hundreds of thousands of people worldwide. Clinical research relies on volunteers like you to help develop safer, more effective treatments.

Each participant’s contribution brings researchers closer to breakthrough therapies that might reduce symptoms, improve skin health, and enhance overall quality of life.
