The Power of Clinical Trials

Clinical trials are the crucial link between laboratory discoveries and real-world applications. They allow researchers to evaluate the safety and efficacy of new medical treatments, ensuring that only the most effective and safe options reach the public. At Altus Clinical Research, we specialize in conducting Phase I, Phase II, Phase III, and Phase IV clinical trials, offering a wide range of opportunities for volunteers to participate in studies that can make a significant impact on healthcare.

Why Volunteer for Clinical Trials?

Volunteering for clinical trials is a rewarding experience that offers several benefits:

  • Contribution to Medical Advancements: By participating in clinical trials, you play a vital role in helping researchers develop new treatments that could improve the lives of millions.
  • Access to Innovative Treatments: Volunteers often have the opportunity to receive new therapies before they become widely available, which can be particularly beneficial for those with conditions that have limited treatment options.
  • Personalized Care: Our team at Altus Clinical Research provides personalized attention to each participant, ensuring a safe and supportive environment throughout the trial process.

Current Studies at Altus Clinical Research

We are currently conducting several studies that address common health issues, including:

  • UTI Study: You may be eligible to join the study if you are a female aged 18 or older experiencing UTI symptoms like frequent or urgent urination, painful or burning urination, and/or lower abdominal pain, with symptoms lasting 1-4 days, and have not taken antibiotics for a UTI in the past 7 days.
  • Hot Flash Study: You may be eligible to join the study if you are a healthy menopausal woman aged 40-65 years and experience moderate to severe hot flashes daily.
  • Psoriasis Study: You may be eligible to join the study if you are a man or woman aged 18-75 years and have moderate to severe plaque psoriasis.
