What to Expect at Altus Clinical Research

For Palm Springs residents considering internal medicine clinical research, here is a general overview of what the process typically looks like at Altus Clinical Research.

Your experience begins with a consultation or pre-screening conversation, during which our team reviews your health history and the requirements of any currently enrolling studies. If you appear to meet initial criteria, you will be invited for a formal screening visit at our Lake Worth facility. Our experienced investigative team monitors participants carefully throughout the duration of each trial. You may withdraw from a study at any time without penalty. Individual eligibility and experiences vary, and our team is available to answer your questions at every stage.
