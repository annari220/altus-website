Altus Clinical Research – Hot Flash Study

March 5, 2025

At Altus Clinical Research, located in Lake Worth, FL, and Laurel, MS, we are passionate about advancing personal health through clinical trials. Our current hot flash study aims to explore innovative treatments for menopausal women experiencing this common symptom.

Understanding Hot Flashes

Hot flashes are sudden feelings of warmth, often most intense over the face, neck, and chest. They’re typically accompanied by sweating and rapid heartbeat, and can last from a few seconds to several minutes. Hot flashes are primarily related to fluctuating hormones during menopause, particularly dropping estrogen levels.

Why Do Hot Flashes Occur?

Research suggests that declining estrogen levels can disrupt the brain’s temperature regulation. The hypothalamus, our body’s internal thermostat, becomes more sensitive to slight temperature changes. When it perceives the body as too warm, it triggers a series of cooling mechanisms – the hot flash.

Who Can Join the Study?

You may be eligible to participate in our hot flash study if you meet the following criteria:

  • Healthy menopausal women aged 40-65 years
  • Experience moderate to severe hot flashes daily
  • Other requirements will apply

Why Participate in Our Hot Flash Study?

By volunteering for this clinical trial, you can:

  1. Contribute to advancing medical knowledge about menopause and hot flashes
  2. Receive personalized care from our expert research team

The Power of Clinical Trials

Clinical trials like our hot flash study are crucial for developing new therapies. They allow researchers to evaluate the safety and efficacy of potential treatments, ensuring only the most effective options reach the public.

At Altus Clinical Research, we specialize in conducting various phases of clinical trials, offering volunteers the opportunity to make a significant impact on healthcare.
